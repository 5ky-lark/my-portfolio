# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary users are recruiters, hiring managers, technical interviewers, and potential clients evaluating Skylark for web development work. Secondary users are peers and collaborators exploring project depth and engineering approach. Users typically browse quickly on desktop or mobile to assess credibility, project impact, and communication clarity.

## Product Purpose
This portfolio exists to convert attention into professional opportunities by clearly presenting skills, experience, and proof-of-work projects. Success means visitors can quickly understand strengths, trust implementation quality, and take a concrete next step (contact, resume download, or deeper project review).

## Brand Personality
Confident, clean, human. The voice should feel professional and direct, with enough warmth to stay approachable. The experience should communicate technical competence without feeling robotic or over-produced.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics (card-heavy, templated sections, repetitive blocks) and avoid overly flashy neon or effect-heavy visuals that distract from project substance. The portfolio should feel intentional and modern, not trend-chasing.

## Design Principles
1. Show proof, not claims: lead with concrete projects, outcomes, and capabilities over abstract self-description.
2. Reduce decision friction: make contact, resume access, and project exploration obvious and low effort.
3. Prioritize scanability: support fast first-pass evaluation with clear hierarchy and concise copy.
4. Balance polish with authenticity: polished execution should reinforce trust while preserving a personal voice.
5. Maintain focused motion and effects: visual flair supports comprehension and tone, never competes with content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Baseline target is WCAG 2.1 AA across the full portfolio experience, including keyboard navigation, readable contrast, semantic structure, and accessible labeling for interactive elements (including chat interactions). Respect user comfort by avoiding excessive motion and ensuring content remains usable across devices and input methods.
